WHAT NODI DOES

Give your agent a budget.
Keep the final say.

Nodi controls what an AI agent is allowed to do. Give the agent a mission, set its budget, and decide which actions need your approval.

Before an action runs, Nodi checks those rules. It allows actions within the limits, pauses actions that need your approval, and blocks actions outside the mission. Each decision is recorded so you can review what happened.

  1. Define the missionChoose what the agent may do and how much it may spend.
  2. Check each actionNodi checks the mission, permissions, and spending limits.
  3. Stay in controlApprove requests, revoke permission, and review the activity history.
NOW TRY AN EXAMPLE

An agent is buying a computer monitor.

You tell it: “Spend at most $300, and ask me before spending more than $250.”

First, try a $450 monitor. Then try a $275 monitor and follow the approval process. See how the same rules handle each purchase.

No sign-up or tokens. Your own temporary workspace. All purchases are simulated; no real money is spent.

Open your Nodi service in Render → Environment. Copy each secret into its matching field below. These are the two secrets you generated when deploying.

Use NODI_ADMIN_TOKEN for the administrator and NODI_AGENT_TOKEN for the agent. Keep both private. You will need to reconnect after reloading this page.
